HOW TO PLAY

🏫 You're a googly at Googly High. High school lasts 3 years — that's one hour of play. Each year has 6 school days, and every day follows the bell schedule: before school, three classes, lunch, and after school.

🔔 When the bell rings, get to your next class (follow the gold marker and the map). Be late and you're tardy. Don't show up and you skipped — and if Principal Googlesworth catches you in the halls, it's off to his office.

🤔 Everything is a decision. Friends, crushes, bullies and teachers will walk up to you. Answer the teacher's questions in class. Choose where to sit at lunch. Pull the fire alarm… or don't. After each choice you'll see if it was the BEST or the WORST decision.

📈 Your grades (GPA), popularity, conduct, energy and money all change. 3 detentions = a suspension. 3 suspensions = expelled. Big days: tests, the Homecoming Dance, the Talent Show, the Championship Game, Prom — and Graduation.

⌨️ WASD walk · Shift run · Space jump · Mouse look (click the game first) · E use / talk / sit · 1–4 pick a choice · Tab report card · Esc pause. The game saves itself every 30 seconds.
